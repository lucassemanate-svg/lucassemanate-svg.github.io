Our Bikes

Haight. Durability and high style are the hallmarks of the Haight road bike. A pavement specific build will give you a comfortable, steady ride on the toughest city streets. A stylish steel frame reduces vibration. 18-speed drivetrain helps out on hills. Robust tires enable some offroad excursions without sacrificing the resistance you need for street riding.

Marina. Venture out with the Marina road bike designed to go the distance. A classic steel frame and a plethora of road-tested features make this a bike for in or outta town. Responsive handling and all-terrain tires give you options off the beaten track. And if you are merely heading cross town, you'll appreciate the 27-speed drivetrain for those SF hills.

Mission. Whether you live in the Mission, or you're just on one, this durable and affordable bike will turn your commute into a joy ride. Wide tires, a flat-bar setup, and 21-speed gears give you a solution for a wide range of conditions at a price that few can beat. Looking to customize your ride? Rack and fender mounts come standard on all models.

Richmond. The Richmond is geared to longer rides, offering a combination of comfort and handling with an upright rider position. A carbon frame and durable construction are characteristic of high performance, yet lightweight road bike that that's perfect for whiling away the weekend. You'll be planning longer trips with your best of best compadres.

Sunset. Can you get performance and comfort? Right here with the Sunset, our fitness-oriented road bike. A lightweight, aluminum frame provides efficient shock absorption. Durable components and a 22-speed drivetrain gives you reliable traction on hills and valleys. Disc brakes offer superior stopping power under a wide range of weather and road conditions.

SoMa. Commuting is a breeze on the SoMa road bike. An aluminum frame with internal cables offers a long-lasting, durable foundation for city streets. Direct-pull brackish help you stop in an instant. A wider tire enhances traction and comfort without any sacrifice in speed or performance. Sporty and versatile, your ride won't languish at the weekends.
